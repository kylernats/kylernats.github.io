About

Cybersecurity analyst working where banking regulation, cloud infrastructure, and AI meet.

I'm a cybersecurity analyst at Sunflower Bank, where I lead secure AI enablement across more than 1,500 enterprise users. In practice that means answering an uncomfortable question every day: what happens when a language model with access to customer data receives input from someone who wants it to misbehave?

Most of my work sits in three places. Detection — I've authored 55+ alert runbooks across SIEM and EDR environments and built the enterprise business email compromise playbooks. Governance — I own a three-tier access exception framework that processes 40+ tickets a week and closed the segregation-of-duties bypasses that existed before it. AI security — DLP controls and data-loss policies for Microsoft Copilot that keep non-public information from leaking under GLBA.

Before that I was a security operations analyst at FirstBank, investigating live threats across 2,500+ endpoints in Splunk and CrowdStrike Falcon, and a graduate research analyst at the University of Arizona's McKeever MIS Lab, where I ran a gap analysis of 58 critical controls against NIST 800-53 and built an ELK stack to centralize log data.

Why this site exists

Nearly everything I do professionally is unpublishable — it belongs to the banks I do it for. So this portfolio is the parallel version: the same problems, rebuilt independently in environments I own, with every step documented.

The rule I've set for it is that each project has to start from a problem a real business would pay to solve. Not a tutorial, not a hello-world deployment. Real infrastructure, deployed for real, with the security layer it would genuinely need — then torn down with the receipts kept.

Outside of security

I founded the University of Arizona's first bodybuilding-focused club and grew it to 180+ members, and I've spent time teaching cybersecurity basics to K-12 students and volunteering at the FutureCon security conference. I graduated magna cum laude with a 3.8 GPA and finished my M.S. in MIS with a concentration in Information Security & Assurance in May 2026.
