GRC & Risk

Turning frameworks into control decisions someone can actually act on — and defend in an audit.

Governance work gets dismissed as paperwork right up until an auditor asks a question nobody can answer. The pieces here are about making controls concrete: what the framework asks for, what's actually implemented, and what the gap costs to close.

Grounding

What this is built on

Grounded in production experience: a formal gap analysis of 58 critical security controls against NIST 800-53 at the McKeever MIS Lab, and ownership of a 3-tier access exception framework (Manager → IT Risk → CISO) at Sunflower Bank processing 40+ weekly tickets at 100% audit readiness. Frameworks: NIST 800-53, NIST CSF 2.0, NIST AI RMF, CMMC 2.0, SOC 2, MITRE ATT&CK. Certified OCEG IAIP.
