GRC & Risk

Turning frameworks into control decisions somebody can actually defend in an audit.

Governance gets written off as paperwork until an auditor asks a question nobody can answer. The work here is about making controls concrete: what the framework asks for, what's actually in place, and what closing the gap costs.

I've written up CMMC 2.0 and the NIST AI RMF so far.

At work and school: a gap analysis of 58 controls against NIST 800-53 at the McKeever MIS Lab, and a 3-tier access exception framework at Sunflower Bank handling 40+ tickets a week. Frameworks: NIST 800-53, CSF 2.0, AI RMF, CMMC 2.0, SOC 2, MITRE ATT&CK. Certified OCEG IAIP.
