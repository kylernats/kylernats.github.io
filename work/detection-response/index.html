Detection & Response

Detection rules, triage pathways, and the incident response playbooks that turn an alert into a decision.

Detection work is the part of security I've spent the most real hours on — but almost none of it can be published, because it belongs to the banks I did it for. What goes here instead is equivalent work rebuilt from scratch in my own lab, against traffic and telemetry I generate myself.

Grounding

What this is built on

Grounded in production experience: 55+ alert runbooks across SIEM and EDR at Sunflower Bank (35% MTTR reduction), 10+ incident response playbooks at FirstBank, live triage across 2,500+ endpoints in Splunk and CrowdStrike Falcon, and an ELK deployment at the McKeever Lab. Employer-owned material stays unpublished; the methodology is rebuilt here independently.
